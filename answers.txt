
Q1: Pros and Cons of Agile Development?
    Pros:
        Faster shipping time
        Adaptability to change throughout the entire process
    Cons:
        Lacks detailed documentation
        Less predictable

Q2: Responsibilities of a Scrum Master vs Project Owner
    Scrum Master:
        Runs the meetings
        Fosters communication
        Supports the team
        Maintains tools & removes the blocking factors
    Project Owner:
        Sets the project goals 
        Approves the major decisions
        Assigns team responsibilities
        Makes sure the project is on track with the expectations

Q3: What are the 3 types of meetings that happen during sprint cycle? List at least
2 things on what needs to be discussed during these meetings.
    Sprint Planning:
        - The goals and expectations of the sprint
        - The deadlines and the assignments for the team members
    Daily Scrum:
        - What everyone accomplished the day before
        - What everyone plans to accomplish that day
    Sprint Review:
        - Additional work & tasks for the product
        - What could be done differently

Q4: Differentiate between Epic, Story, Bug and Tasks types in JIRA.
    Epic: Large parts of the project that can be broken down into smaller stories.
    Story: Short requirements/request written from the perspective of the end user.
    Bug: Problems that need to be fixed.
    Task: Tasks that need to be done for the epic to be completed.
